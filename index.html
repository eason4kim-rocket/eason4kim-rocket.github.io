03 / WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

The model interprets the scene. The Action Contract decides whether the robot may move.

PHYSICAL ROOTS

RGB, depth, and forwarded Claims from one capture count as one physical observation.

ACTION CONTRACT

Evidence must match the robot, payload, corridor, action, time window, calibration, and root requirement.

BELIEF GAP

A denial names what is missing. A fixed, auditable ranking selects a new view that targets the gap instead of repeating the same one.

PURIFY GO

Purify is not another model. It is a separately compiled Go contract gate; direct motion requires Python and Go to agree.

PASSIVE

Deny and detour

It stays safe when evidence is weak, but pays for uncertainty with a longer route in every world.
